My story

My name is Ojolade Victor Oladamilare. I'm from Oyo State, Nigeria.

I studied Mathematics Education at the Federal University Oye-Ekiti (FUOYE) and earned a B.Sc.Ed.

Alongside school, I learned practical skills. I install solar systems, I do electrical house wiring, and I design graphics.

I'm now studying cybersecurity at MIVA Open University. I'm passionate about safeguarding the online space, and I'm an avid learner who enjoys picking up new skills.
